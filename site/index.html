A small app.
A shorter feedback loop.

The macOS app includes its service and Node.js runtime. You bring your coding client and a Feishu bot.

  1. Install on your Mac.

    Download the DMG, drag Nootify into Applications, then open it from there. On a new installation, the app installs and starts its background service.

  2. Connect Feishu and your clients.

    Configure a Feishu bot and its chat permissions, then enable the clients you use on the Agents page. Follow the setup guide for credentials, trusted senders, and client connections.

  3. Start a conversation.

    New agent conversations receive instructions to open a Feishu group and post updates. Reply in that group to continue the matching session on your Mac.

A few things to know.

Does Nootify run a model for me?

No. It connects to the Claude Code, Codex, or OpenCode client you already run. It does not call a model API or replace your coding agent.

Can I reply while my Mac is offline?

The local service and the target session must be available. Nootify does not revive archived or inactive sessions, and a delivered message does not automatically approve a permission request.

Where does the data go?

Progress updates and replies travel through your Feishu group. Service state, routing information, and credentials are stored locally on your Mac. The public website does not connect to your local service.

What happens when I close the app?

Closing the window hides it. Quitting the desktop app leaves the independent background service running. Service controls in the menu bar let you manage it separately.
